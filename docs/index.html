What's inside

Everything you need to
capture and annotate

A complete annotation toolkit with share-ready output, running entirely on your Mac — no accounts, no cloud, and nothing leaves your device.

Instant capture

Drag to select any region, with a live size readout, or press Space to capture an entire window or screen — triggered by a global hotkey and fully aware of multi-monitor setups.

Screen recording

Press a hotkey, then record a region, a single window, or the entire screen directly to MP4 — with a floating timer, pause/stop controls, and optional system or microphone audio.

Annotate in place

The editor opens directly over your capture, offering pencil, marker, arrows, shapes, text, numbered steps, blur, spotlight, and emoji stamps.

Resize & crop

Drag any of eight handles to adjust the region — trim it in, or expand it to capture more of the screen — plus crop, rotate, and flip. Annotations are baked in, so every export stays sharp.

Screen ruler

Place a pixel ruler over your capture — resize it with the arrow keys, move it to measure any distance, then click to record the reading.

Zoom in on your pixels

Magnify any region into a crisp callout. Everything stays in full-resolution image space, so the inset — and your export — remains sharp.

Pin to screen

Float any capture above every application and across Spaces. Drag to reposition, or use a corner handle to resize — ideal for reference while you work.

Copy text & QR

Extract selectable text or decode a QR code directly from any screenshot, using on-device OCR powered by Apple Vision.

Share-ready backgrounds

Place your capture on a polished, padded frame with shadow and rounded corners — choose from ten presets or a custom colour, rendered at full resolution.

Before / after GIF

Combine an original and an edited capture into an animated before-and-after GIF with a single click — generated with ImageIO, no additional tools required.

Rebindable hotkeys

Global shortcuts for screenshots and recording, fully customizable in Settings. Configure your save folder, format, quality, and auto-copy behaviour.

Private & lightweight

No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry — every capture remains on your Mac. Built in Swift with zero dependencies, running quietly in the menu bar.

Get started

Installing m_capture

1

Download the DMG

Download the latest m_capture.dmg from Releases and open it.

2

Move it to Applications

Drag m_capture into your ~/Applications folder. Because the app isn't notarized, macOS will block it on first launch — open Terminal and run cd ~/Applications && xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine m_capture.app, then double-click m_capture to launch it.

3

Grant Screen Recording

On first capture, macOS asks for Screen Recording permission. Approve m_capture under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording and relaunch.

How it works

From a hotkey to your
clipboard in a few seconds

01

Press a hotkey

Press ⌃⇧S for a screenshot or ⌃⇧R to record — a dimmed selection overlay appears instantly, with no application to launch.

02

Drag a region

Drag to select any region, with a live size readout, or press Space for whole-screen mode. Fully aware of multi-monitor setups.

03

Annotate & deliver

The in-place editor opens directly over your capture. Press ⌘C to copy or ⌘S to save.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is m_capture free?

Yes — m_capture is open source and MIT-licensed, free for anyone to use.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 14 (Sonoma) and later. It lives in the menu bar, with a Dock icon for quick access.

Why does macOS block it on first launch?

It isn't notarized by Apple yet. Move it into ~/Applications and clear the quarantine flag from Terminal — see Get started above.

Where are my captures saved?

To your chosen folder (the Desktop by default) and the clipboard. Set the format and location in Settings.

Does it send my screenshots anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your Mac — no accounts, no cloud, no telemetry.

Can I change the keyboard shortcuts?

Yes — rebind the screenshot and record hotkeys under Settings → Shortcuts.
